07 · Limitations and future scope

Scope of the evidence and next steps

Limits of the present evidence

01

Evidence from one acquisition

One L-band UAVSAR scene over flat Rosamond lakebed supports the comparison; model rankings may change in other scenes.

02

Range-based calibration statistics

Range-line averaging can mix scattering populations. Low cross-pol SNR and small denominators affect Quegan’s estimates.

03

Stages tested as separate modules

Point-target and distributed-target stages were evaluated separately; their combined accuracy remains to be measured.

04

Terrain effects remain untested

The flat site does not test terrain orientation, changing local incidence angle, or complex forest and urban structure.

What should be tested next

01

Broaden independent validation

Test more acquisitions, incidence angles, land covers and bands. Quantify bias and uncertainty using independent references.

02

Make estimation sensitive to data quality

Test local windows, SNR/coherence masks and regularization. Check stability without suppressing real scene variation.

03

Validate the complete processing chain

Connect the modules; assess cumulative error with surveyed reflectors, HV/VH consistency and scattering signatures.

04

Evaluate terrain-aware processing

Test DEM-based geometry and orientation diagnostics on high-relief scenes; separate terrain effects from calibration errors.

Validation priority: a correction should improve channel consistency while preserving independently known radiometry and scattering behavior.
