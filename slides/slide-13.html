Channel imbalance: balancing H against V

Same paths as the “Why calibration” diagram ↗. Black: injected tone → C paths → receivers; it bypasses the target and antenna paths.

Separate the phase contributions

After cross-talk removal; assume reciprocity and negligible Faraday rotation.

ψHH = φTh + 2φAh + φRh
ψVV = φTv + 2φAv + φRv

The A path is traversed twice: out to the antenna and back from it. Define each relative phase as H − V.

Δco = arg ⟨OhhOvv*⟩ − φco,true
= φT + 2φA + φR

With an internal tone · Lou & van Zyl

Measure the receiver; infer the transmitter

φcal = φR + φC ⇒ φR = φcal − φC
δ = arg ⟨OhvOvh*⟩ = φR − φT ⇒ φT = φR − δ

Measure stable φA and φC during installation. A common-source tone tracks receiver drift; reciprocity supplies δ from scene data.

Without an internal tone · Zebker & Lou

Use a known target phase instead

s = Δco − 2φA = φT + φR
φR = (s + δ)/2   ;   φT = (s − δ)/2

Use a trihedral (φco,true ≈ 0) or a suitable known-phase area, plus measured φA. Average complex products before taking their phase.
