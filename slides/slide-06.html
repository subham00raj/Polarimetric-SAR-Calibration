01 · Fundamentals

A long aperture from motion and Doppler history

1

One target, many radar positions

The target remains in the antenna beam over a flight distance Lsa. Each pulse records a complex echo with a different slant range and phase.

2

Motion creates a Doppler history

Range decreases on approach, is smallest at broadside, then increases. The corresponding Doppler sweeps from positive through zero to negative.

φ(t) = −4πR(t) / λ
3

Azimuth compression focuses the target

A matched filter compensates the predicted phase history. Echoes for the same location add coherently, concentrating energy into a narrow azimuth response.

Lsa ≈ Rλ / L
δa ≈ Rλ / (2Lsa) ≈ L / 2

L: physical antenna length. Ideal full-aperture stripmap result; weighting and processing affect practical resolution.

Sources: thesis Chapter 1, Azimuth Resolution in SAR; https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/s3fs-public/2024-06/sci-sar-userguide.pdf .
