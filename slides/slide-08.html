Polarimetric SAR: four channels per pixel

Resultant electric field and its locus

E(t) = ah cos(ωt) ĥ + av cos(ωt + Δ) v̂

At a fixed point, follow the tip of the resultant E: it traces a line, a circle, or an ellipse.

The helix shows the wave across space; its fixed-point polarization locus is circular.
