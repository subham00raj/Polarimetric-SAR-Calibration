Resolution: can the radar distinguish two targets?

Rangeslant range: inclined radar-to-target direction

Two targets return echoes at different times.
A shorter pulse keeps those echoes separate.

δr = cτ / 2 Shorter pulse τ
finer range resolution

Ground range: δg = cτ / (2 sin θ). Here, θ is the incidence angle.

Azimuthazimuth: parallel to the flight path

A wide beam illuminates nearby targets together.
A narrower beam distinguishes them as the radar moves.

δa = Rβ = Rλ / L Narrower beam β
finer azimuth resolution

Real aperture, at the same range R. A longer antenna L narrows the beam.
