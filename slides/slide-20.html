Why does Quegan’s α grow?

Scene correlations can bias the leakage estimates. At low cross-pol SNR, noise adds power without a matching contribution to X.

αQ = corrected HV power / X

A small |X| makes the ratio unstable; the one-pass estimator has no damping or rescaling.
