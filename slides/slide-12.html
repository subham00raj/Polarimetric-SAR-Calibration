Radiometric calibration with corner reflectors

Why integrate instead of taking the peak?

A point target produces a focused impulse response across several pixels. Pixel spacing is often finer than resolution; integrating its power is less sensitive to focusing than the peak value. This does not require the physical CR to be larger than the resolution cell.
