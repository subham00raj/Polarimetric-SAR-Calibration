Why two hardware matrices?

Gain, phase delay and H/V leakage arise during both transmission and reception. T and R describe these separate responses; their parameter values need not be equal.

Why does FΩ appear twice?

The wave crosses the ionosphere twice. Faraday rotation is non-reciprocal: in this convention the same FΩ acts on each leg. For airborne UAVSAR, Ω ≈ 0 and both matrices reduce to I.

Why multiply the matrices?

Each stage mixes or scales the two H/V field components. Successive linear transformations multiply; read the equation from right to left. Hardware H→H gains are normalized to 1 and absorbed into K.
