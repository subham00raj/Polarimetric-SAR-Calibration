SAR imaging geometry

Side-lookingSlant rangeAzimuth = flight direction
1

Active microwave sensor

Carries its own illumination: images day and night, through cloud and smoke.

2

Records amplitude and phase

Each pixel is complex: A cos φ, A sin φ, located by its slant range.

3

Rich information channels

Intensity, polarization and frequency each encode surface structure and moisture.

4

Shown in decibels

Intensity is displayed on a log (dB) scale; quality is governed by the signal-to-noise ratio.
